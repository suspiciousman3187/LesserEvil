What to include in a bug report

  • LesserEvil version (shown in the title bar / about dialog).
  • Windower version + a list of other addons loaded.
  • The character / zone where the issue happened.
  • Repro steps - what you clicked, what you expected, what happened.
  • Anything in the LesserEvil window that looked off (screenshots help a lot).
